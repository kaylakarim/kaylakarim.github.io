Take your Child to Work Day
×

With this project, I wanted to create vibrant designs infused with a playful, childlike theme, incorporating doodle elements and whimsical imperfections to capture the essence of the event. I first designed the flier, which was distributed to both children and parents. The flier outlined the details of the upcoming scavenger hunt, and In addition to the flier I designed scavenger hunt cards for the kids. These cards guided them from one clue to the next in their adventurous quest. All of these assets were designed using Adobe Illustrator.
